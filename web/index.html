Two people. Two AI proxies. Each proxy acts only inside a sealed Intent Card, tags every claim as stated, sourced or assumed, and stops to ask its human when it reaches a limit. What comes out is a decision brief you can explain without opening the chat history.

01 · intent

Seal an Intent Card

Write your brief in plain language. Claude drafts the card: goal, must-haves, what you may concede, hard limits, when to ask you. You confirm it and it is sealed with a hash. The other side never sees it.

02 · provenance

Every claim is tagged

Stated by my human, sourced from a document, or assumed. Assumptions stay amber until a human confirms them, and repeating the other side's claim never upgrades it. That is how a cascade gets caught.

03 · accountability

Escalate, then brief

At a limit, a proxy stops and asks one concrete question. The agreement ships with a brief: terms, whose authority covers each one, the assumptions it still rests on, and a hash-chained ledger.
