How it works

  1. 1

    Tell your AI what you want

    What you need, what you're fine with, and what's off the table. Only you see this.

  2. 2

    The two AIs talk it through

    You can watch. If something goes beyond your instructions, your AI stops and asks you.

  3. 3

    You get a clear summary

    What was agreed, why each point was allowed, and anything nobody confirmed.

Why not just let two AIs chat?

AIs fill gaps with confident guesses, and another AI will often agree with them. Behalf marks every guess, so it can't slip into your deal unnoticed.
